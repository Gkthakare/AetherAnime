# Vision

## The product

AetherAnime turns finding and holding onto anime into **arriving somewhere**. The user should feel they crossed a threshold into a world, oriented themselves in it, and travelled to a destination — not that they filtered a grid.

Every decision answers one question: *does this make the world feel more real?*

## Spatial model

The product is a place, and its language is spatial and consistent:

| Concept | Meaning | Surface |
|---|---|---|
| Threshold | the invitation to cross | Home (`/`) |
| World | the place you stand in | `/world/aetheranime` idle |
| Crossing | a route deeper into the world | world regions (continuum, thresholds ahead) |
| Destination | a specific anime, arrived at | `?anime=` inside the world |
| Path | somewhere to go from a destination | Story, Signals, Kinship |
| Threshold out | leaving for the official source | Watch Now |

Vocabulary matters. Copy says *arrive*, *cross*, *path*, *destination*, *presence* — never *results*, *cards*, *items*, *browse*.

## What AetherAnime is not

Not a streaming site. Not a dashboard. Not a recommendation feed. Not a chatbot with a background image. Not a catalog with atmosphere sprinkled on top.

## Truth constraint

The world is imaginative; the data is not. Synopses, titles, years, genres, studios and relationships come from real providers. Nothing about a real anime is invented, embellished, or LLM-rewritten. Watch Now only ever points at a verified official destination. Unknown availability is shown as unknown, never as unavailable and never faked.
