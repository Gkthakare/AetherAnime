# TASK-103 — Vertical Chamber Universe

Status: FROZEN (implementation complete)
Area: Destination universe composition · poster-only chamber grammar

## Decision

**Arrived Destination is a vertical chamber universe: Arrival → Story → World → Record → Paths → Beyond as full-viewport volumes formed from `CanonicalAnime.poster`, not a cinematic details page.** Architecture from TASK-096→101 stays; TASK-103 changes visual grammar only.

## Why

TASK-102 confirmed the Destination loop behaves as a world but still read as stacked content over soft poster fields. Capital visual phase requires immersive chamber scale under a strict poster-only artwork boundary.

## Protected behaviour

- Artwork remains `CanonicalAnime.poster` only — no Class C plates, galleries, generated imagery, or artwork APIs
- Chambers are full-viewport volumes (`min-height: 100dvh`); arrival figure is full-bleed environmental material
- `data-universe-here` ownership is consequential (active field ≈0.72–0.88, inactive ≈0.08); TASK-098 explore reasserts after depth recession including mobile
- One atmosphere: when poster artwork is present, WorldEnvironment geography subordinates further (`world-destination-presence.css`) without a second environment system
- Transport, warp, network, `journeyOrigin`, Memory, Watchlist, analytics, native scroll, reduced motion, and TASK-046 continuous budget unchanged
- Contract: `anime-destination.chamber.test.ts` plus existing destination suites

## Implementation area

`anime-destination.universe.css` · `anime-destination.chamber.test.ts` · `anime-destination.constants.ts` (`POSTER_SIZES`) · `world-destination-presence.css`

## Contracts

Reduced motion keeps chamber identity and field ownership; collapses transition travel. Keyboard/focus and spatial index anchors preserved. Production Chromium: settled Destination has no continuous compositor; A→B hop (warp+remount+enter) measured ~42 FPS avg at 1440 (p95 ≈60ms) after scale restraint — one-shot ceremony, not a continuous breath. Idle FPS not re-claimed here.

## Do not undo

- Do not restore side-plate Arrival figure or webpage section stacking as the primary Destination grammar
- Do not weaken here/explore opacity ownership back to decorative subtlety
- Do not reopen poster-only by shipping environmental masters inside this decision — that requires a separate art-asset track
- Do not add Chamber Engine, scroll hijack, WebGL, or a second atmosphere pipeline

## Poster-only ceiling (documented, not solved)

True character-in-environment staging and unique architectural places per title cannot be guaranteed from portrait poster crops alone. Extreme chamber crops trade figure recognition for volume. Future art-asset track needs provenance, licensing, fallback, responsive, and loading contracts.
