# TASK-105 — Immersive Dimensional Traversal Ceremony

Status: FROZEN (implementation complete; not deployed)
Area: World → Anime arrival ceremony · dimensional depth over TASK-104 seam grammar

## Decision

**World→Anime travel remains a one-shot cyan event-seam / threshold-aperture ceremony, but reads as dimensional traversal — environmental compression, converging trajectories, monumental hollow aperture, and early canonical-poster reveal through the threshold — not a thin slit/portal widget.** TASK-103 Destination chambers and transport lifecycle remain unchanged.

## Why

TASK-104 fixed portal brand grammar (cyan seam, no warm accretion) but peak ceremony still read as a weak vertical slit over black. Capital gap was immersion (depth, env participation, destination leak, scale), not a new transport or Destination redesign.

## Protected behaviour

- TASK-104 cyan event-seam grammar: no warm amber/orange portal bloom; no conic spinning accretion; no TASK-031 cyan oval-ring language
- Layer slots + `world-warp-reveal` when `atmosphere.poster` is set; `data-warp="black-hole"`; `DURATION.WARP` (2.4s); one-shot CSS; `EnvironmentCrossingFrame` / `aether-warp-env`
- Horizon aperture is a luminous rim around a **hollow** core so the poster field can leak through before ceremony completion
- Poster comes only from validated `CanonicalAnime.poster` via `worldArrivalAtmosphere` — no new artwork API
- Contracts: `world-realm-crossing.immersion.test.ts` (+ ceremony + crossing suites)

## Implementation area

`world-realm-crossing.css` · `world-realm-crossing.view.tsx` · `world-arrival.atmosphere.ts` · `world-realm-crossing.immersion.test.ts`

## Contracts

Accessibility: decorative `aria-hidden`, pointer-events none, reduced motion acknowledgement without travel. Performance: one-shot ceremony only. Do not claim continuous ≥55 FPS during hop.
